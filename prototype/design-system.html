Colors

Terracotta and clay tones drawn from istanbul's historic rooftops and stonework — terracotta is the single primary accent; a warm clay tone carries freshness/highlight duty; help/alerts get their own red, deliberately not a shade of the other two. Replaces the earlier pastel-purple pass per the 2026-07 product-owner review.

Map markers

Final mvp freshness thresholds (docs/product/map.md): today <24h, this_week 24h–7d, this_month 7d–30d, long_not_seen ≥30d or never updated. Each state pairs a color with a second, non-color cue (ring weight, opacity) so it doesn't rely on hue perception alone. Needs-help always wins over freshness or selection styling.
