Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 8, 2026

Tekir ("we", "us") operates the Tekir mobile app and the website at tekir.istanbul, a map for finding, following, and helping Istanbul's street cats. This policy explains what data we collect, why, and what stays private versus what is public by design.

Tekir is a Turkey-based, Turkish-market product. We process personal data under Turkey's Personal Data Protection Law (KVKK, Law No. 6698), and, where applicable to users in the European Economic Area, the General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR). References to KVKK below are not optional boilerplate — KVKK is the primary law that applies to this app.

Data we collect

Phone number

We collect your phone number to authenticate you via one-time passcode (OTP), sent through our SMS provider, Twilio. Your phone number is used for sign-in, account recovery, abuse prevention, and to verify account ownership. It is never shown publicly and never included in public API responses.

Device location

If you grant location permission, your device's current location is used client-side to center the map on your area and to query cats near you. We do not store a history of your location, and your location is never published or shown to other users. Location is used only while you use the map or discovery features.

Content you submit

Cat locations, photos, videos, status updates, needs-help alerts, and comments that you submit are public by design — this is how Tekir works as a shared, up-to-date picture of the city's street cats. Anyone can view this content, including people who are not signed in. Photos and videos are re-encoded on upload, which strips EXIF and other embedded metadata (including camera location) before storage.

Display name

Your display name is public and shown next to updates, photos, and other contributions you make.

Push notification tokens

If you enable notifications, we collect a Firebase Cloud Messaging (FCM) device token so we can deliver notifications about cats you follow. This token is never public.

Device identity

We collect a device identifier for abuse control — for example, to associate activity across accounts on the same device when investigating misuse. A device identifier is never sufficient to authenticate as a user on its own.

Analytics

We use Firebase Analytics as an opt-in product analytics provider to understand how the app is used. Analytics events are limited to a fixed, closed set defined in the app's own codebase and never include your name, free text, precise location, tokens, or raw record identifiers. No analytics user ID is set, and we do not use advertising identifiers, ad personalization, audiences, or session replay.

What we don't do

  • We don't sell your personal data.
  • We don't expose your phone number, device tokens, or device identifiers to other users or to advertisers.
  • We don't publish a history of your location.
  • We don't use your data for ad targeting.

Account deletion

You can delete your account from within the app. Deleting your account removes or anonymizes your personal account information, such as your phone number and display name association. Public cat records and contribution history remain, as they are shared city information, but are no longer associated with your public identity.

Your rights under KVKK and GDPR

As a Turkey-based service, we process personal data in line with KVKK. If you are located in the EEA, GDPR also applies to our processing of your data. Under these laws you can generally request access to, correction of, or deletion of your personal data, and object to certain processing. To exercise these rights, contact us at privacy@tekir.istanbul.

Contact

Questions about this policy or your data: privacy@tekir.istanbul. General feedback: hello@tekir.istanbul. Support: support@tekir.istanbul. Security issues: security@tekir.istanbul.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy as the product changes. Material changes will update the "Last updated" date above.
