# cats istanbul

## product definition

cats istanbul is a map-first product for discovering and following street cats in istanbul.

the map is the primary experience.

every cat on the map represents a real street cat at a real location.

every feature should strengthen the map instead of competing with it.

## problem

information about street cats is fragmented, temporary and difficult to find again.

people often remember seeing a cat but cannot easily answer:

- where is the cat usually found?
- what is its latest status?
- does it need help?
- is the information still current?

cats istanbul exists to make this information easy to discover on a map.

## core user goals

- find out where a specific cat can be found.
- see the latest known status of a cat.
- know how to help a cat that needs it.
- trust that the information reflects reality.

## product principles

- the map is always the primary experience. no feature should pull attention away from it.
- freshness matters more than volume. a small amount of current information is worth more than a large amount of stale information.
- trust is the product. every feature must protect the reliability of what the map shows.
- helping a cat matters more than any engagement metric.
- community contributions are the source of truth, but the product is responsible for keeping that information trustworthy.

## what the product is not

- it is not a social network. it is not built for feeds, follows or engagement loops.
- it is not a place for conversation unrelated to a cat's location or status.
- it is not a general-purpose pet or animal app. it is specific to street cats.
- it is not a rescue or shelter management system.

## success

success means that people can quickly discover reliable, up-to-date information about street cats and contribute new information whenever something changes.
