# product review — july 2026

a one-time review of the product discovery workshop (see the `qa` transcript and the docs it produced: [[vision]], [[principles]], [[users]], [[map]], [[cats]], [[updates]], [[alerts]], [[trust]], [[community]], [[discovery]], [[notifications]]). this is a snapshot, not a decision — it does not change any of those docs on its own.

## duplicated decisions

- cat/update staleness shows up in two places: [[cats]] says a cat becomes inactive after a long silence, [[updates]] raises "about 1 year with no update" as the same idea. no single confirmed rule.
- the login-to-post rule is stated in both [[trust]] (login needed to add media, updates, or a cat) and [[alerts]] (same rule, repeated for alerts). not wrong, just stated twice.

## conflicting decisions

- [[alerts]] treats "needs help" as its own alert type, separate from a normal update. [[principles]] later suggests it might just be a special kind of update. these two docs disagree and it is not resolved.

## weak decisions

- cat becomes "inactive" after a long silence: answered with "i think" (bence), no threshold defined.
- phone verification: justified informally ("in case someone uploads something inappropriate"), no clear policy behind it.
- "too many notifications won't be a problem": stated as an assumption, not tested with users.
- "most important features for year 1": answered with "maybe," not firm.

## missing information

- minimum information required to add a cat: never answered.
- how we'll know the product is successful: unsure, only a rough idea (1,000 cats added).
- how long an alert should last: undecided, somewhere between 3 hours and 1 day.
- whether filters beyond "needs help" are needed: undecided.

## potential risks

- no "alert resolved" state, and notifications are not location-based — someone could travel to help a cat that's already fine.
- phone verification sits right at the first-cat-add step, the exact moment the product needs contribution to be as easy as possible.
- no minimum info requirement plus a deferred duplicate-cat merge process — early risk to map quality and trust.
- gamification (badges, points) is fully undecided, but "1,000 cats added" is the only success signal on the table — there's no defined lever to actually drive that number.

## recommendations for discussion with the product owner

- resolve the "needs help" conflict between [[alerts]] and [[principles]] before that flow gets built.
- pick one staleness threshold instead of the two hinted at in [[cats]] and [[updates]].
- define the minimum fields required to add a cat.
- decide if phone verification at first-add is worth the friction it adds.
- sharpen the success metric beyond "roughly 1,000 cats."
