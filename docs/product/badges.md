# badges

## goal

celebrate real-world contribution without turning tekir into a competitive social network.

## decisions

- badges recognize useful contributions to street-cat information and care.
- badges do not unlock product capabilities or grant moderation authority.
- badges are permanent once earned.
- there are no points, streaks, daily missions, public rankings, or leaderboards.
- badge progress is based only on authenticated contributions.
- profiles show earned badges near the profile header, with contribution totals and recent public contributions.
- the initial mvp badge vocabulary and thresholds are:
  - `first_sighting`: post the first `seen` update.
  - `feeder`: post 5 `fed` updates.
  - `water_helper`: post 5 `water_provided` updates.
  - `neighborhood_watcher`: post `seen` updates for 10 distinct cats.
  - `cats_of_istanbul`: contribute to 25 distinct cats through updates, media, or cat creation.
- a badge can be earned only once.
- badges cannot be hidden individually in mvp.
- badge thresholds may change only for future earnings; already-earned badges are not revoked merely because a threshold changes.

## open questions

- none for mvp.

## out of scope

- points or spendable rewards.
- competitive rankings.
- badge levels or seasonal badges.
- trusted-contributor status derived from badges.
